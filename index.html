Our Story

About Mirrors N Scissors

Founded in 2018, Mirrors N Scissors is the bridal beauty studio of Sanskriti, a makeup artist known for refined, elegant, and camera-ready looks that stay true to the beauty of the individual.

01
The Artist Behind the Brand
Sanskriti, also known as Faces By Sanskriti, is the lead artist of the studio. Trained under Leena Bhushan at Looks Academy, she brings a polished, subtle aesthetic rooted in North Indian bridal artistry.
02
Signature Style
Her signature approach is “subtle, less is more, classy” — soft glam, luminous skin, and graceful details designed to make every bride feel naturally radiant and beautifully herself.
03
Luxury Bridal Experience
From engagement and reception to destination weddings, the studio offers premium makeup, hair styling, saree and lehenga draping, and bridal finishing that feels elevated, comfortable, and long-lasting.

Crafted for Beautiful Beginnings

Mirrors N Scissors is recognised for its premium product selection, destination wedding expertise, and dedication to creating looks that feel timeless and personal. With 3 awards to its name and a love for modern bridal elegance, the studio brings couture-level beauty to every celebration.

  • Professional bridal & party makeup
  • Premium international beauty brands
  • Destination wedding coverage across India
  • Travel to the client’s venue
